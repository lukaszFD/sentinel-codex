Sharing on LinkedIn

Every news item in the digest e-mail gets a Udostępnij na LinkedIn link. It opens a new LinkedIn post with the title, the article link and the hashtags below; nothing is published until you press Post. On a phone with the LinkedIn app the link opens the app.
  • A hashtag applies to news of its topic; Both applies to every item.
  • Lowest order first, at most {{ setting_label('share_max_hashtags') }} (now {{ cfg.get('share_max_hashtags')|num }}).
  • Write without #, CamelCase reads better: ThreatIntel.
  • Disable instead of delete: seed hashtags come back after a redeploy.
